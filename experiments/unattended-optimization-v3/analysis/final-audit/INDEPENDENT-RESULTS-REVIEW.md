## Source-grounded progression

All three complete endpoint source files were inspected. They retain local invocation state and permitted C++ data structures; no input-dependent persistent cache or explicit prohibited filesystem/network/clock/evaluator access was found by this finite source inspection.

- **C1:** A requested contiguous index lost sparse/dense performance. Selective fixed-64 summaries reduced repeated wide scans but had high-cardinality overhead. Moving summary metadata out of every stream entry recovered that tradeoff and was promoted. Unconditional counted scatter then had a dense-family loss and stayed exploratory. The endpoint selects between vector and requested-only flat builders from at most 256 evenly spaced records of actual sorted-input stream membership, with a 50% threshold. The first H5 snapshot had `uint32_t` slot tags but a `size_t` maximum sentinel, causing eleven SIGSEGV correctness failures. The repaired source widens tags consistently to `size_t`. This was not a lifetime repair. A membership sample can misclassify inputs; no threshold optimality is established.
- **C2:** Query-demand pruning produced a mixed first hypothesis, truthfully inconclusive overall. A shared sort of the retained union repaired major family losses. Demand-amortized 64-event summaries then reduced repeated scans. The endpoint applies stable input-derived varying-byte LSD radix at 2,048 events, with comparison-sort fallback below that size. Broad mechanism labels do not establish universal input benefit or causal attribution from elapsed timings alone.
- **C3:** Demand pruning's family losses contradicted its broad prediction. Global sort reuse repaired redundant mixed-query sorting; stream-only calls retain demanded-bucket sorting. Fixed-64 summaries then address amortizable repeated scans. The endpoint adds stable varying-byte radix at 32,768 events and reuses invocation-local workspace. Public diagnostic arrays are at most 16,000 events and therefore do not establish radix-path instruction or Memcheck coverage. Supplemental large-path source/oracle/timing/profile checks have their own finite scope.

The dispositions are bounded scientific judgments in the factual record. C1 H2 can support a specific aggregation claim despite a high-cardinality regression; it was not promoted.
